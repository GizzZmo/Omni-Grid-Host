# Greetings from Jon-Arve Constantine

Jon-Arve Constantine is the operator Face-Recon expects to see. These lines are his. They are not flavor text glued on after the fact. Each one belongs to a moment on the desk: boot, a linked sidecar, a bad voicing, a rose blip on the radar, a grid you rearranged at 1 a.m. and then blamed on the software.

Use them as splash lines, status whispers, commit messages, or the first sentence of a session note. Pick at random. If a line does not fit the hour, it will fit tomorrow.

> "The grid is awake. I am Jon-Arve Constantine. If the rain is moving, you are already inside."

## How to use this list

Treat the bullet lists as a deck.

1. Note the moment: boot, audio, sidecar, trouble, done.
2. Take the first line that would still be true if someone else were sitting in the chair.
3. If you need one at random, count the lines in the section and take `session minute mod count`.
